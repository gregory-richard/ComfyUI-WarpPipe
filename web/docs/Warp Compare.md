# Warp Compare

Compare two images with a slider.

## Overview

**Warp Compare** shows two images on top of each other and lets you drag a slider to move between them. If you connect batches, the arrows step through the pairs: before 1/after 1, before 2/after 2, and so on. The sides are labelled "before" (left) and "after" (right). It replaces rgthree's image comparer in WarpPipe workflows.

## Inputs

Both inputs are **optional**:

- **before** — The earlier image, shown on the left (IMAGE).
- **after** — The later image, shown on the right (IMAGE).

## Output

None. This is an output node and shows the images as temporary previews.
